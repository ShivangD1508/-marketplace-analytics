# Kids deck v9 — consolidation brief

The working instructions used to turn v8 (35 slides) into v9. Use them again for later rounds.

## Goal
A clear, consolidated deck: **title + 6 core slides** that hit the big points. Everything else goes to the appendix and is **moved, not deleted**.

## Scope rule
Only two v8 slides decide what goes in the core:
- **v8 slide 2: client follow-up asks** (8 asks)
- **v8 slide 3: executive summary** (5 insights, each with a starting point)

If a chart, table or number does not directly answer one of those asks or prove one of those 5 insights, it goes to the appendix.

The birthday-party and coupon asks get a one-line answer on slide 2 and point to the appendix. Neither finding is big enough for its own slide.

## Rules for every core slide
1. **Action title** that states the finding, not the topic.
2. **At most two visuals.** Each one is a native chart with large data labels and no gridlines, with the Kids bar highlighted in purple and peers in gray.
3. **A one-sentence reading guide under each chart** says what to notice.
4. **A "What to do" bar** carries the starting point from the exec summary.
5. **One denominator per chart, named in the chart label.** Do not show the same population as a % of two different totals unless a visual spells out both totals.
6. **A source note** in small type, with pointers to the appendix slides (A#) that hold the detail.
7. Leave out numbers that do not change the decision: customer counts to the unit, medians *and* means, 40%/60% threshold sensitivities, full peer tables.

## The 16% / 56% confusion (client feedback)
- **Lead with one denominator: all new Michaels customers (17.0M).** A waterfall goes 17.0M → 2.71M with Kids in the first basket (16%) → 1.26M where Kids was at least half the basket (7%).
- The 56% (2.71M ÷ 4.82M first-time Kids buyers) appears only as a small side visual. It shows two bars that share the same purple 2.71M block over two different totals, and is labeled as describing *who* Kids first-timers are, not how many new customers Kids brings in.
